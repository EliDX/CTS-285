## Assumptions
- All ingredients listed are currently present and within reach:
    - 2 slices of white bread 
    - 8oz jar of peanut butter 

- All dinnerware/utensils requested for use are also present:
    - butter knife
    - 1 plate of any material.

- The individual/robot following these instructions is assumed to have the following:
    - 2 hands, or other equivalent appendages
    - the ability to open a jar
    - the ability to accquire the necessary materials as listed above.
    - the necessary space to construct the sandwich.

## Steps
- First, locate the pieces of bread.
- Grab both pieces of bread.
- Place one piece of bread onto the left side of the plate.
- Place the remaining piece onto the right.
- Locate and grab the jar of peanut butter, then place it in front of yourself.
- With one hand, secure the jar in place.
- Place your other hand around the lid of the jar. 
- Firmly twist the hand holding the lid in a counter clockwise motion. This should open the jar.
- Let go of the jar and place the lid down after removing it from the jar.
- Locate the butter knife, and scoop a large amount of peanut butter from the jar.
- Spread the peanut butter (which is not on the knife) onto the left slice of bread until the surface is evenly coated.
- Set the butter knife down.
- Grab the slice of bread which is not coated in peanut butter, and place it on top of the other slice.

## Done When
- Two pieces of bread are in contact with eachother, with peanut butter seperating them.
